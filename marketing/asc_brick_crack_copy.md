Brick Crack — the classic block-stacking puzzle, reimagined with neon polish and a daring twist.

REMEMBER THAT BLOCK GAME?
You stacked falling shapes, tried to clear rows, lost yourself in the rhythm. Brick Crack honors that ritual — same satisfaction — but tuned for 2026: snappier controls, vibrant tile colors, particle effects on every clear, and an option to break the formula completely.

CLASSIC MODE
Pure, polished, no surprises. Seven standard piece shapes, the official 7-bag randomizer (no more streaks of the same piece), levels that ramp from gentle to relentless, and a save system that resumes your run exactly where you left off.

EXTENDED MODE — NEW ABILITIES
Clear lines, fill your ability bar, and your next piece becomes a power-up:
• Bomb — explodes a 3×3 area, dramatic particle flash
• Laser — rotates to fire a horizontal or vertical beam, clears an entire row or column

Strategy meets satisfaction. Zen rhythm with moments of pure action.

POLISHED FEEL
• Tap to rotate, swipe to move, hold to soft-drop, fast swipe-down to hard-drop
• Ghost piece shows exactly where your piece will land
• Hard-drop streak effects in the piece's color
• Line clear flash + particle burst
• Screen shake on heavy landings
• Smooth synth background music + crisp SFX (full volume control)

CLEAN & FAIR
• Free to play with light, respectful ad placement
• 90-second gap between interstitials — no spam
• Optional Remove Ads in-app purchase — gameplay is unchanged
• Saved progress carries over after purchase

BUILT BY ONE PERSON
Ekimoz Games is a solo studio. Brick Crack started as a personal love letter to the block puzzle of my childhood, the one we all played on cheap LCD handhelds. Eight months of iteration, hundreds of small details. If you find a bug or have an idea, write to us — we read every email.

ORIGINAL DESIGN
Brick Crack is an original take on the falling-block genre. We do not use any trademarked names or branded assets from other games.

FOLLOW US
